# Ankobra Port Users' Association · notice to members
Date: Wednesday 2026-11-04, 16:00

The strike of dock workers at the Ankobra terminal continues. No vessel was loaded on Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday. Containers at the terminal remain in the terminal.

Talks between the terminal operator and the dock workers' union resume on Thursday morning. The association expects loading to restart early next week.

We will inform members as soon as there is news.
